# Code of Conduct

## Project Unchained

### 1. Our Commitment

Project Unchained is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, professional, and collaborative environment for everyone participating in its activities.

We welcome contributors regardless of nationality, ethnicity, gender, professional background, disability, religion, political beliefs, or level of experience.

Our mission is built on freedom of expression, digital rights, privacy, transparency, and responsible research. These principles must also guide our interactions with one another.

### 2. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with dignity and respect.
- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and reasonable disagreement.
- Provide honest, accurate, and verifiable information.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Acknowledge the contributions of others.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Follow applicable laws and project policies.
- Support a safe and productive collaborative environment.

### 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are prohibited:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate misinformation, fabricated evidence, or impersonation.
- Persistent disruption of project activities.
- Abuse of administrative permissions.
- Retaliation against individuals who report misconduct in good faith.
- Unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or infrastructure.
- Attempts to manipulate contributor reviews or project decisions through intimidation.

### 4. Professional Disagreement

Disagreement is an important part of independent research.

Participants are encouraged to challenge assumptions, question conclusions, and propose alternative interpretations.

However, criticism must focus on ideas, evidence, and methodology rather than personal characteristics or attacks.

### 5. Reporting Violations

Anyone experiencing or witnessing unacceptable behavior is encouraged to report it privately to the project maintainers.

Reports should include:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant dates and context.
- Supporting evidence, if available.
- The preferred method of follow-up.

Reports should not expose sensitive personal information publicly.

### 6. Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and determining appropriate responses.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

1. Informal warning.
2. Formal written warning.
3. Temporary restriction of participation.
4. Removal of inappropriate content or contributions.
5. Suspension of repository permissions.
6. Permanent removal from the project.

Serious misconduct may result in immediate suspension while an investigation takes place.

### 7. Confidentiality and Fairness

Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion.

Individuals involved should be treated fairly, and decisions should be based on available evidence.

Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance, particularly when disclosure is required to address serious risks or comply with applicable law.

### 8. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Pull Requests and Issues.
- Official project communication channels.
- Project meetings and events.
- Other activities conducted in an official Project Unchained capacity.

### 9. Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing these standards consistently and without discrimination.

Project leadership does not exempt anyone from this Code of Conduct.

### 10. Final Statement

Project Unchained believes that freedom of expression and mutual respect must coexist.

We encourage open debate, independent thinking, and responsible collaboration.

Every contributor shares responsibility for maintaining a professional and respectful community.

**Project Unchained — Knowledge should be open. Research should be independent. Freedom should not be taken for granted.**
