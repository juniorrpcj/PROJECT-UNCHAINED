# Contributing to Project Unchained

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Project Unchained**.

Project Unchained is an open initiative dedicated to researching, documenting, and addressing challenges related to censorship, digital freedom, government surveillance, information control, and fundamental rights in the digital age.

We believe that an open, transparent, and collaborative environment is essential to protecting freedom of expression and promoting unrestricted access to information.

Whether you are a researcher, developer, cybersecurity specialist, journalist, human rights advocate, legal expert, or concerned citizen, your contribution can make a difference.

This document explains how to participate, submit contributions, and apply to become a specialist contributor.

---

## 1. Our Principles

All contributors are expected to support the following principles:

- **Freedom of Expression:** Support the fundamental right to express opinions and access information.
- **Transparency:** Promote openness, accountability, and verifiable research.
- **Independence:** Maintain intellectual independence and resist political or commercial manipulation.
- **Privacy:** Respect personal privacy and protect sensitive information.
- **Non-Discrimination:** Treat all contributors with dignity and respect, regardless of nationality, background, beliefs, or political views.
- **Responsible Research:** Conduct research ethically, legally, and without causing unnecessary harm.
- **Open Collaboration:** Encourage knowledge sharing, constructive criticism, and cooperation.

Project Unchained is an independent initiative and does not endorse political parties, governments, or ideological movements.

## 2. Who Can Contribute?

We welcome contributions from individuals and organizations with relevant knowledge, experience, or interest in our mission.

Potential contributors include:

- Software developers and open-source engineers.
- Cybersecurity and privacy specialists.
- Digital rights and human rights researchers.
- Journalists and investigative researchers.
- Legal professionals specializing in digital rights and civil liberties.
- Academic researchers and university students.
- Data analysts and technology policy researchers.
- Translators, editors, and technical writers.
- Digital activists and civil society organizations.

You do not need professional experience to contribute to every area. Genuine interest, responsible participation, and a willingness to learn are equally valuable.

## 4. How to Contribute

There are several ways to participate.

### Step 1: Explore the Repository

Before contributing, review the repository, existing documentation, open issues, and ongoing projects.

### Step 2: Identify an Opportunity

Look for tasks labeled:

- `good first issue`
- `help wanted`
- `research`
- `documentation`
- `security`
- `enhancement`

You may also propose new ideas through GitHub Issues.

### Step 3: Discuss Your Contribution

For significant changes, open an issue before beginning work.

Explain:

- What you intend to contribute.
- Why the contribution is relevant.
- How you plan to approach the task.
- Any potential risks or dependencies.

### Step 4: Submit Your Contribution

Submit changes through a GitHub Pull Request.

Your submission should include:

- A clear and descriptive title.
- A summary of the changes.
- The motivation behind the contribution.
- Relevant references or supporting evidence.
- Testing information, when applicable.

### Step 5: Review and Feedback

Contributions may be reviewed by project maintainers or designated specialists.

Be prepared to answer questions, make revisions, and incorporate constructive feedback.

Acceptance is not automatic and depends on quality, relevance, and compliance with project guidelines.

## 5. Specialist Application Process

Project Unchained is interested in building a multidisciplinary network of qualified specialists.

Individuals with relevant professional experience, academic backgrounds, or demonstrated technical expertise are encouraged to apply.

### 5.1 Specialist Categories

Applications are welcome in the following fields:

| Category | Relevant Expertise |
|---|---|
| Software Engineering | Programming, infrastructure, open-source development |
| Cybersecurity | Security research, threat analysis, vulnerability assessment |
| Digital Privacy | Privacy technologies, data protection |
| Digital Rights | Freedom of expression, internet governance |
| Legal Research | Constitutional law, international human rights |
| Journalism | Investigative journalism, fact-checking |
| Academic Research | Political science, technology policy, social sciences |
| Data Analysis | Data science, statistical analysis, digital investigations |

### 5.2 Application Requirements

Candidates should provide:

1. Full name or preferred professional name.
2. Professional email address or another reliable contact method.
3. Area of specialization.
4. Relevant professional or academic background.
5. A brief description of their experience.
6. Portfolio, GitHub profile, publications, or relevant work samples, if available.
7. Motivation for joining Project Unchained.
8. Preferred level of participation.

Candidates are not required to disclose sensitive personal information or provide government identification as part of the initial application.

### 5.3 Application Submission

To apply, open a GitHub Issue using the **Specialist Application** label, if available, or contact the project maintainers through the official communication channels listed in the repository.

Do not publish private contact information, confidential documents, or sensitive personal details in public issues.

Applications may also be submitted through a dedicated application process if one is established by the project.

### 5.4 Application Review

Applications will be evaluated based on:

- Relevant knowledge and experience.
- Demonstrated expertise or potential.
- Alignment with the project's principles.
- Professional integrity.
- Ability to collaborate constructively.
- Commitment to responsible research.

Selected candidates may be invited for further discussion or a technical evaluation.

### 5.5 Specialist Recognition

Approved specialists may receive recognition within the project, subject to their consent.

Possible forms of recognition include:

- Contributor or specialist listing.
- Acknowledgment in research publications.
- Participation in specialized working groups.
- Technical review responsibilities.
- Invitations to collaborate on specific initiatives.

Specialist status does not automatically grant administrative permissions or authority over the project.

## 6. Code of Conduct

Every participant must contribute to a respectful and professional environment.

The following behaviors are unacceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on personal characteristics or beliefs.
- Deliberate misinformation or fabricated research.
- Impersonation or misrepresentation of qualifications.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Malicious code or intentionally harmful contributions.
- Attempts to manipulate project governance or community decisions.
- Threats, targeted harassment, or coordinated abuse.

Disagreements are welcome when expressed respectfully and supported by reason or evidence.

Violations may result in warnings, removal of contributions, suspension, or permanent exclusion from the project.

## 7. Research Integrity and Verification

Project Unchained values accuracy and verifiable evidence.

Contributors conducting research must:

- Use reliable and identifiable sources whenever possible.
- Distinguish verified facts from allegations, opinions, and hypotheses.
- Provide citations and supporting documentation.
- Avoid manipulating data or presenting misleading conclusions.
- Respect applicable laws and ethical research standards.
- Correct errors when they are identified.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Anonymous sources may be used when necessary to protect individuals, but their information must be handled carefully and independently assessed.

## 8. Security and Responsible Disclosure

Security research is welcome, provided it is conducted responsibly.

Contributors must not:

- Access systems without authorization.
- Exploit vulnerabilities against third parties.
- Steal, modify, or expose private information.
- Disrupt services or infrastructure.
- Publish exploitable vulnerability details before responsible coordination when doing so could create unnecessary harm.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately to the maintainers through an officially designated security contact.

If no security contact has been established, request a private reporting channel rather than publishing sensitive technical details.

## 9. Intellectual Property and Licensing

Unless otherwise specified, contributions submitted to this repository are subject to the project's published license.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

- You have the right to submit the material.
- Your contribution does not knowingly violate third-party intellectual property rights.
- You have identified relevant external sources and licenses.
- You agree to the repository's contribution and licensing requirements.

Contributors retain any rights not explicitly granted under the applicable license.

Please review the repository's `LICENSE` file before submitting code or other licensed material.

## 10. Communication and Collaboration

Project Unchained encourages open and constructive communication.

GitHub Issues should be used for:

- Bug reports.
- Research proposals.
- Feature requests.
- Documentation improvements.
- Specialist applications, when appropriate.

Pull Requests should be used for:

- Code contributions.
- Documentation updates.
- Research improvements.
- Technical corrections.

Keep discussions relevant, professional, and evidence-based.

Avoid duplicate issues and respect the time and effort of volunteer contributors.

## 11. Governance and Project Responsibilities

Project Unchained may designate maintainers, reviewers, researchers, and specialists to coordinate specific activities.

Responsibilities are assigned based on demonstrated competence, reliability, and project needs.

Maintainers reserve the right to:

- Review and approve contributions.
- Request modifications.
- Reject contributions that do not meet project standards.
- Moderate community discussions.
- Manage repository permissions.
- Establish additional policies when necessary.

Project roles are not political appointments, professional certifications, or guarantees of employment.

## 12. Volunteer Participation

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, participation in Project Unchained is voluntary and unpaid.

Contributors are not employees, representatives, or legal agents of the project solely because they participate.

No contributor may make official statements on behalf of Project Unchained without appropriate authorization.

## 13. Reporting Problems

If you encounter misconduct, security concerns, conflicts of interest, or violations of these guidelines, contact the project maintainers through an appropriate private channel.

Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed according to their seriousness.

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is not acceptable.

## 14. Final Statement

Project Unchained exists because freedom of information, privacy, transparency, and freedom of expression deserve serious attention.

We believe that meaningful progress comes from independent thinking, verifiable research, responsible technology, and international cooperation.

Every contribution matters, whether it comes from an experienced specialist or someone taking their first step into open-source collaboration.

If you share these principles and are willing to contribute responsibly, we welcome your participation.

**Knowledge should be open. Research should be independent. Freedom should not be taken for granted.**
