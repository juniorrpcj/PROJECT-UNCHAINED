# Project Governance

## Project Unchained

### 1. Purpose

This document establishes the governance framework of Project Unchained.

Its purpose is to promote transparency, accountability, responsible leadership, and fair participation in project decisions.

Project Unchained aims to operate as an independent, collaborative, and internationally accessible initiative.

### 2. Governance Principles

Project governance is based on the following principles:

- Transparency in decision-making.
- Independence from political and commercial interference.
- Accountability of project leadership.
- Equal respect for contributors.
- Technical and research integrity.
- Responsible management of resources.
- Protection of contributor privacy.
- Open participation whenever practical.

### 3. Organizational Structure

Project Unchained may operate through the following roles.

#### 3.1 Project Founder

The Founder establishes the project's original mission, vision, and strategic direction.

Responsibilities may include:

- Protecting the project's founding principles.
- Supporting long-term development.
- Appointing initial maintainers.
- Facilitating strategic discussions.
- Promoting institutional independence.

The Founder is expected to respect this governance framework and the rights of contributors.

#### 3.2 Core Maintainers

Core Maintainers are responsible for the daily management of project repositories and activities.

Responsibilities include:

- Reviewing and approving contributions.
- Maintaining repository quality.
- Managing technical priorities.
- Coordinating development activities.
- Enforcing project policies.
- Managing access permissions.

#### 3.3 Technical Specialists

Technical Specialists provide expertise in areas such as:

- Software engineering.
- Cybersecurity.
- Digital privacy.
- Network research.
- Data analysis.
- Infrastructure.

Their responsibilities are defined by their assigned projects and areas of expertise.

#### 3.4 Research Specialists

Research Specialists contribute to:

- Digital rights research.
- Censorship documentation.
- Policy analysis.
- Investigative research.
- Academic publications.
- Fact-checking and verification.

Research conclusions must be supported by credible evidence and transparent methodology.

#### 3.5 General Contributors

General Contributors support the initiative through documentation, translation, development, research, community participation, and other approved activities.

### 4. Appointment of Roles

Project roles should be assigned according to:

- Relevant qualifications or demonstrated experience.
- Previous contributions.
- Reliability and professional conduct.
- Commitment to project principles.
- Ability to collaborate effectively.

Appointments should be documented internally or publicly when appropriate.

No individual should receive elevated permissions solely because of personal relationships or unsupported claims of expertise.

### 5. Decision-Making

Project decisions should follow a transparent and proportionate process.

#### Routine Decisions

Routine technical and administrative decisions may be made by the responsible maintainers.

#### Significant Decisions

Major changes affecting the project's mission, governance, licensing, security policies, or organizational structure should involve consultation with relevant maintainers and specialists.

#### Strategic Decisions

Long-term strategic changes should be discussed with the core leadership and documented with their rationale.

### 6. Voting and Consensus

Project Unchained encourages consensus whenever practical.

For decisions requiring a formal vote:

- Eligible participants must be identified in advance.
- Voting procedures must be communicated clearly.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.
- Decisions and relevant outcomes should be documented.

Unless otherwise specified, decisions requiring a formal vote should be approved by a simple majority of eligible voting participants.

The project may establish additional voting procedures as its community develops.

### 7. Transparency and Accountability

Significant governance decisions should be documented.

Maintainers should communicate relevant changes to affected contributors.

Project leadership should periodically review:

- Project priorities.
- Contributor participation.
- Security practices.
- Governance effectiveness.
- Policy compliance.

Confidential information and personal data should not be published merely in the name of transparency.

### 8. Conflicts of Interest

Contributors and maintainers must disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Examples include:

- Financial interests in organizations affected by a project decision.
- Personal relationships influencing an appointment.
- Undisclosed institutional affiliations relevant to published research.
- Attempts to use project resources for unrelated personal or commercial purposes.

Individuals with material conflicts may be excluded from specific decisions.

### 9. Removal or Replacement of Maintainers

Maintainers may be removed or replaced due to:

- Serious misconduct.
- Repeated violations of project policies.
- Abuse of administrative privileges.
- Extended inactivity.
- Inability to fulfill assigned responsibilities.
- Resignation.

Removal decisions should be based on documented reasons and handled through a fair process.

Emergency access restrictions may be applied when necessary to protect project infrastructure.

### 10. Dispute Resolution

Internal disagreements should first be addressed through constructive discussion.

If a dispute cannot be resolved:

1. The issue should be documented.
2. Relevant parties should be given an opportunity to respond.
3. An impartial maintainer or designated reviewer should assess the situation.
4. A decision should be communicated with appropriate reasoning.

Serious allegations should be handled separately under the Code of Conduct.

### 11. Project Independence

Project Unchained aims to remain independent of political parties, governments, commercial interests, and external organizations.

Cooperation, research partnerships, and financial support may be considered when compatible with the project's mission.

No external party may claim control over the project solely because of a contribution, sponsorship, partnership, or affiliation.

### 12. Amendments

This governance document may be updated as the project evolves.

Proposed amendments should:

- Explain the reason for the change.
- Be shared with relevant contributors.
- Allow reasonable discussion.
- Be approved through the applicable governance process.

The current version published in the official repository is the authoritative version.

### 13. Final Statement

Project Unchained believes that independent initiatives require responsible leadership, transparent decisions, and accountable collaboration.

Governance exists to protect the mission, not to create unnecessary hierarchy.

**Project Unchained — Independent by principle. Transparent by design.**
